SUMMARY
-------
Three FastAPI/Uvicorn inference endpoints on ports 4000, 8000, and 9000
serve Pukaar.ai's pediatric health AI platform without any authentication.
No API key, no bearer token, no session cookie, no IP restriction.
The inference service accepts live user_id + child_id parameters tied to
real production user records. 50,000+ children's health data in blast radius.
